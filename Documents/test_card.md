We believe that: 
UQ Students find the process of choosing courses confusing or time-consuming. & UQ students use multiple scattered sources (course profiles, forums, friends, etc.) when choosing courses.
To verify that we will:
Interview UQ students about their experiences with course selection for this current semester.
We will measure:
How many students report being confused, how many report the process being time consuming and what resources students use for course selection.
We're right if:
80% of UQ Students find specific difficulties with choosing courses being time consuming and confusing & At least 7 out of 10 UQ students report using a variety of sources for course information.
Therefore we will ask:
Q1: What degree and major are you studying?
 
Q2: How was your experience choosing courses this semester?
 
Q3: When choosing a course what do you prioritise and why? (Enjoyment, relevance to study area, useful skills etc.)?
 
Q4:  How long does it take for you to finalise your courses for the semester? 

Q5:  Do you currently use any tools or resources to help choose courses? (e.g., websites, spreadsheets, advice from others) 

Q6: If you could improve the course selection process at UQ, what would you change?
 
Q7: What were the parts of choosing courses that made the process harder for you?
 
Q8: If there was a supplemental app or website to help you find course information, what kind of information would you like on there?
