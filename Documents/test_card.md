# Test Cards

## Hypothesis 1: Choosing Courses is confusing
**HYPOTHESIS**

We believe that: 
UQ Students find the process of choosing courses confusing or time-consuming.

**TEST**

To verify that we will:
Interview UQ students about their experiences with course selection for this current semester.

**METRIC**

We will measure:
How many students report being confused, how many report the process being time consuming.

**CRITERIA**

We are right if:
80% of UQ Students find specific difficulties with choosing courses being time consuming and confusing.

**INTERVIEW QUESTIONS**

Q1: What degree and major are you studying?

Q2: How was your experience choosing courses this semester?

Q3: When choosing a course what do you prioritise and why? (Enjoyment, relevance to study area, useful skills etc.)?

Q4: How long does it take for you to finalise your courses for the semester? 

## Hypothesis 2: Students use a variety of sources from a vareity of places to help course selection:

**HYPOTHESIS**

We believe that:
UQ students use multiple scattered sources (course profiles, forums, friends, etc.) when choosing courses.

**TEST**

To verify this we will:
Interview UQ students about what resources they use when choosing courses.

**METRIC**

We will measure:
How many students report a difficulty with the variety and difficulty in accessing the multiple resources they need when choosing courses.

**CRITERIA**

We are right if:
At least 7 out of 10 UQ students use multiple scattered sources (course profiles, forums, friends, etc.) when choosing courses.

**INTERVIEW QUESTIONS**

Q1: Do you currently use any tools or resources to help choose courses? (e.g., websites, spreadsheets, advice from others) 

Q2: If you could improve the course selection process at UQ, what would you change/what new features would you add?

Q3: What were the parts of choosing courses that made the process harder for you?

Q4: If there was a supplemental app or website to help choose courses, how likely would you be to use it?
