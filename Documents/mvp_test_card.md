# MVP Test Cards

## Hypothesis 1: Students want to filter courses by workload, difficulty, assessment types, and relevance

**RELATED LEARNING CARD**

See: [Hypothesis 1 learning card](./mvp_learning_card.md#hypothesis-1-students-want-to-filter-courses-by-workload-difficulty-assessment-types-and-relevance)

**HYPOTHESIS**

We believe that:
UQ students want to compare and filter courses by workload, difficulty, assessment types, relevance, or similar factors when choosing electives.

**TEST**

To verify that we will:
Interview UQ students about how they choose electives and what course comparison filters they want.

**METRIC**

We will measure:
How many students independently mention workload, difficulty, assessment style, relevance, or a similar comparison factor as something they want when choosing electives.

**CRITERIA**

We are right if:
At least 7 out of 10 students independently mention workload, difficulty, assessment style, relevance, or a similar comparison factor as something they want when choosing electives.

**INTERVIEW QUESTIONS**

Q1: When you are choosing electives, what factors do you usually think about first, and why?

Q2: If you could build your ideal way to compare courses, what filters or comparison options would you want to have?

## Hypothesis 2: Students want a repository of course summaries with prerequisites, assessments, and workload

**RELATED LEARNING CARD**

See: [Hypothesis 2 learning card](./mvp_learning_card.md#hypothesis-2-students-want-a-repository-of-course-summaries-with-prerequisites-assessments-and-workload)

**HYPOTHESIS**

We believe that:
UQ students want a clear repository of course summaries that includes prerequisites, assessments, and workload information before enrolling.

**TEST**

To verify that we will:
Interview UQ students about what information they look for before enrolling in a course.

**METRIC**

We will measure:
How many students mention prerequisites, assessments, or workload as information they actively look for before enrolling.

**CRITERIA**

We are right if:
At least 6 out of 10 students mention prerequisites, assessments, or workload as information they actively look for before enrolling.

**INTERVIEW QUESTIONS**

Q3: Before enrolling in a course, what information do you usually look for, and where do you usually find it?

Q4: What would an ideal course summary include for you, and why?

Q5: What makes course information easy or hard to understand when you are checking a subject?

## Hypothesis 5: Students want course recommendations or peer feedback to help them choose

UQ students want course recommendations or peer feedback to help them decide whether to take a course.

**TEST**

**METRIC**
